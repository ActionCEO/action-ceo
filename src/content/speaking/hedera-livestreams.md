From June to October 2025 I hosted livestreams on Hedera's official YouTube channel. Hedera is the public network behind the hashgraph, governed by a council of global companies. The streams were co-produced by Hedera and Genzio Media.

## The streams

- [RWAs and AI at the Intersection of Blockchain, and Hedera's Role](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bqKZ_Vrt0GA), Jun 17, 2025. With May Chan (CEO, HashPack), Irfon Watkins (CEO, DOVU) and Ed Marquez (Head of DevRel).
- [Scaling Web3: LinkPool and Binary Holdings x Hedera](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pvy05JZvDKQ), Jul 22, 2025. With Jonathan Huxtable (Founder and CEO, LinkPool) and Darpan Keswani (VP of Marketing, The Binary Holdings). Co-hosted with Ed Marquez.
- [Hedera Momentum: Accelerating Growth and DeFi Innovation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mH4kGbdHSMY), Sep 3, 2025. With Ashe Oro (Director of Ecosystem Growth, Hedera Foundation) and Brady Gentile (Co-founder and CEO, Bonzo Finance).
- [Forever Minting the Future](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6f80vs3zWII), Sep 16, 2025. With May Chan (CEO, HashPack), Ray Yamartino (Dead Pixels Ghost Club), Mark Rise (JustPFP) and Maui Rivera (Wild Tigers).
- [Hedera Builders Live: From Zero to One](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tkCMtYJlJCk), Oct 14, 2025. With Tudor Holotescu (CEO, HeadStarter) and Juno (CMO, Orbit). Co-hosted with Ed.
