The DeRec Alliance is a non-profit that maintains Decentralized Recovery (DeRec), an open, royalty-free standard for recovering keys, passwords and other secrets. A secret is split into encrypted shares held by helpers you pick, and enough of those helpers together can restore it. The alliance brings together teams from the Hedera and Algorand ecosystems and beyond.

More on the standard at [derec.org](https://derec.org).
